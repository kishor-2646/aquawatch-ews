    4. PredictRandom Forest risk + disease index
    5. ApproveAdmin confirms the alert

    How AquaWatch Classifies Water Risk

    Step 1: Raw ADC → Calibrated Values

    The ESP32 reads raw analog values (0–4095). These are converted to physical units on the server:

    SensorRaw ADC → CalibratedMethod
    Turbidity4-point piecewise linear interpolationnumpy.interp(raw, [98, 1160, 1760, 1805], [100, 40, 10, 0.5])
    pHLinear from baseline7.0 + (raw − 626) × 0.01, clamped 0–14
    TemperatureFactory-calibrated DS18B20Direct °C reading, rounded to 2 decimals
    Turbidity calibration example: The ESP32 sends turbidity_raw = 1160. Looking up in the table: 1160 ADC → 40.0 NTU (moderately dirty water). At 1805 ADC → 0.5 NTU (clean drinking water). Values between points are linearly interpolated.

    Step 2: ML Risk Classification (Tier 1)

    A Random Forest Classifier (scikit-learn) takes 4 features and predicts Safe or High Risk:

    FeatureSourceNote
    pHFixed at 7.0pH probe unreliable; uses nominal value
    Turbidity (NTU)Calibrated from ADCPrimary contamination indicator
    Temperature (°C)DS18B20Pathogen growth amplifier
    Dissolved O₂Fixed at 15.0No DO sensor installed
    Example: Turbidity = 40 NTU, Temp = 29 °C → features scaled → Random Forest predicts class 0 → High Risk – Outbreak Warning.

    Step 3: Disease Vulnerability Index (Tier 2)

    For each pathogen, a rule-based score (0–100) uses two environmental indices:

    • TurbIndex: 0 at ≤1 NTU → 1 at ≥50 NTU (piecewise linear)
    • TempIndex: 0 at ≤20 °C → 1 at ≥32 °C (linear)
    DiseasePathogenFormulaTemp Relation
    CholeraV. cholerae100 × TurbIdx × (0.5 + 0.5 × TempIdx)Warm = more risk
    TyphoidSalmonella100 × TurbIdx × (0.6 + 0.4 × TempIdx)Warm = more risk
    DysenteryShigella100 × TurbIdx × (0.6 + 0.4 × TempIdx)Warm = more risk
    GastroenteritisE. coli100 × TurbIdx × (0.5 + 0.5 × TempIdx)Warm = more risk
    Hepatitis AHAV (virus)100 × TurbIdx × (0.6 + 0.4 × (1−TempIdx))Cool = more risk
    Example: Turbidity 40 NTU → TurbIndex ≈ 0.72. Temperature 29 °C → TempIndex = 0.75.
    Cholera: 100 × 0.72 × (0.5 + 0.5 × 0.75) = 63%. Hepatitis A: 100 × 0.72 × (0.6 + 0.4 × 0.25) = 50% (cooler water favours virus survival).

    Air Detection

    If the sensors are not submerged (turbidity raw 1350–1680 AND pH raw < 560), the system labels the reading "Sensor Not Submerged" and skips the ML model to avoid false alarms.
